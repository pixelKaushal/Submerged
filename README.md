## About
**SUBMERGED** is a 2D survival game where players control a fish in a dynamic aquatic ecosystem. Navigate through three unique environment zones, collect floating food to boost your score, and stay alive against relentless predators like sharks, crabs, and kingfishers.

---

## Features
* **Dynamic Day/Night Cycle:** Seamless background color and lighting transitions every 10 seconds.
* **Three Distinct Zones:** Navigate between Sky, Water, and Ground levels.
* **Dynamic Enemies:** Avoid Sharks (Water), Crabs (Ground), and King Fishers (Sky).
* **Food & Scoring System:** Collect Algae (+10 pts) and Meat (+20 pts) to build your score.
* **Animated Environments:** Moving clouds, underwater bubbles, and interactive wave motions.
* **Custom Audio & Graphics:** Full sound effects and background music for menus and gameplay.
